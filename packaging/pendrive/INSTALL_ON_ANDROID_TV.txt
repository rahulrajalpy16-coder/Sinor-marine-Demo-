Installing Sinor Marine Simulator on Android TV / Google TV (offline)

Needs: Android 7.0 or newer, about 10 MB free, a file manager app on the TV.

1. Plug this pendrive into a USB port of the TV or TV box.
2. Open the TV's file manager. Open the USB drive, then the folder Android_TV.
   No file manager on the TV? One (for example "X-plore File Manager") must be added once from
   Google Play, or by your IT administrator, before the event. This is the only step that may need internet.
3. Select SinorMarine-1.0.0.apk.
4. The TV says installs from this source are not allowed: choose Settings, switch on
   "Allow from this source" for the file manager only, press Back, then Install.
5. If Google Play Protect offers to scan the app, you may let it. The app has no internet permission.
6. Open "Sinor Marine Simulator" from the apps row (it shows the Sinor banner).

Remote: arrows move the yellow focus ring, OK presses, Back closes panels and finally leaves the app.
The camera pad at the bottom right turns and zooms the 3D view.

To update later: install the newer APK the same way; saved scenarios are kept.
To remove: Settings > Apps > Sinor Marine Simulator > Uninstall.

Signing certificate SHA-256 is listed in Version_Information\VERSION.txt.
